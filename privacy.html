Privacy information

DryLap Sync is a personal workflow maintained by Shawn Dryer. Setup is in progress. The following describes its intended operation and its current authorization and reporting components.

Google data and its use

The local program accesses file names, identifiers, timestamps, contents, and other metadata needed to compare designated local and Google Drive folders. It uses Google Drive to store files, backups, and reports, and Google Sheets to record activity. Native Google documents may be exported into local reference formats. These operations require the account owner's authorization.

Where information goes

Working files and backups are stored on the owner's computer and Google Drive. Logs can contain paths, filenames, timestamps, conflicts, and report links. For exception review, selected evidence—including file differences and relevant content—may be sent to OpenAI through Codex. Such review is optional processing for this workflow; normal comparisons do not require AI. OpenAI's terms and the owner's account data settings govern that processing.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally provided to unrelated parties. OAuth credentials are kept outside synchronized folders and excluded from review reports. They are not published on this website. Use of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The intended backup policy retains five recent successful backups and two monthly master generations on each side. Evidence for unresolved incidents is preserved. Logs and reports remain until the owner removes them. The owner can stop the local program and revoke Google access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase existing local files, Drive backups, or previously submitted AI review material.

This website

This static website contains no analytics scripts, account sign-in, forms, or file uploads. Its hosting provider may process standard request information, such as an IP address, for delivering and securing the site. The sync program and its credentials are not hosted here.
